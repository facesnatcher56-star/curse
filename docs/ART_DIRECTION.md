# Art direction: not decided yet

The owner is still working out Curse's visual style. **There is no approved art direction right now**, so don't treat any
style (dark fantasy, low-poly, PS2-era, painterly or otherwise) as a project rule.

Until this page says otherwise:
- Follow the owner's instructions for the task at hand. If the look of something is unclear, ask the owner.
- Keep the general readability rule in `AGENTS.md`: effects and art need clear contrast so they read in their real gameplay
  context.
- The practical model rules (Blender export, skeleton, naming, materials and triangle budgets) still apply; they're in
  [art/PIPELINE.md](art/PIPELINE.md).
